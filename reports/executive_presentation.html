The Operational Challenge: Dual Margin Bleed
Slide 2 of 5 • Diagnosis

🚨 Stockouts on Best-Sellers

Top furnishings and appliances run out before replenishment arrives from suppliers. Lead times (21–42 days) were ignored, losing high-margin customer orders permanently.

📦 Cash Locked in Slow Movers

Over 170 SKUs carry more than 14 weeks of supply, with several holding nearly a year of stock. This ties up ₹37.5 Crores in non-liquid working capital in the central warehouse.

FORESIGHT replaces manual spreadsheets with automated forecasting and dynamic reorder thresholds tied to actual supplier lead times.
